METHOD & BOUNDARY

Data, coordinates and geometry.

Fixed parameters, catalogue provenance and the conditions intentionally excluded from this first release.

What Skyward is

V0 provides explainable geometry candidates. It does not determine operational readiness or approve an observation.

AVAILABLE NOW

Current functions

Browse supported catalogues, inspect all-sky and local-field geometry, view source details, and calculate centre and full-footprint windows under explicit geometric constraints.

PRODUCT GOAL

Decision support

Provide read-only, reproducible and explainable evidence that helps an operator or scientist prepare an observing decision.

FUTURE DEVELOPMENT

Evidence not yet connected

After authoritative inputs and validation are available, future versions may add live pointing and device telemetry, weather and atmosphere, mechanical trajectory limits, instrument availability, and joint-observation evidence.

How to use Skyward

  1. Open the sky map.Return to “Sky map & planner” and open the catalogue selector inside the sky-map area.
  2. Select catalogue layers and confirm.The page loads the checked catalogues and draws their markers only after you confirm the selection.
  3. Choose a target and conditions.Select a source, set the time range and geometric constraints, then calculate the windows.
  4. Interpret the result within scope.Green, yellow and red describe geometry only. Check weather, device state, mechanics and operating approval separately.
